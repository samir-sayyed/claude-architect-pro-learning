## Technical qualifications and source corrections

- [Prompt caching](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching): lifetime runs from the start of a writing or reading request and refreshes on reuse. Response streaming consumes that window. Eligibility and rates vary; no universal minimum or price is tested.
- [MCP connector](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/mcp-connector): the inspected connector supports tool calls, rather than every protocol resource feature. Q29 distinguishes a resource-capable client from an intentionally authorized read tool.
- [Batch processing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/batch-processing): count and serialized-size limits both apply; completion may expire, so typical delivery does not guarantee a deadline. Q40 defines decimal MB and omits envelope overhead explicitly.
- [Claude Code permissions](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions): matching deny precedes ask, which precedes allow; specificity does not reverse that order. Q61 supplies all effective matches, avoiding any assumption about command-pattern syntax. Its missing exhibit was caught and repaired before release.
- Token counts are estimates of supported input; context fit does not promise output quality. Structured syntax, business validity and authorization remain separate checks.
- Voting independence is not a performance guarantee; the questions use measured results. Pairwise judge ordering and discarded inconsistent cases require explicit treatment. A bundled experiment can estimate its bundle effect without identifying an individual component contribution.
- Legal and contractual cases stipulate applicability, approved routes and the responsible owner. Shared branding is not evidence of universal BAA coverage. No unverified model-catalog, regional-availability or blanket HIPAA claim is required to solve the form.
