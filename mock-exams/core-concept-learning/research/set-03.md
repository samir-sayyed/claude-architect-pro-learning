## Technical verification and corrections

Primary sources below were actually opened, with relevant sections inspected during authoring. Source links in each question identify the guidance used; the scenario-specific architecture and decision are original applications, not quotations or claims that a document prescribes every implementation detail.

- **Control versus classification:** The videos sometimes describe classifier-plus-code behavior as a guarantee. A deterministic branch can guarantee what happens *given a verdict*, but a classifier can miss the underlying condition. Q42 explicitly handles flagged, indeterminate, timeout, and malformed outcomes; Q43 assumes a missed attack and constrains capability separately. The opened injection guidance supports untrusted tool-result boundaries, least privilege, screening, and red-team tests.
- **Consequential approvals:** Reading third-party material does not automatically make every subsequent harmless action require approval. Q43 tests narrow filesystem/destination capability instead of blanket post-browsing confirmation. Q47 binds approvals to the actual immutable action and distinct authorized custodians; these are explicit scenario policy requirements, not universal legal rules.
- **Model and price claims:** The videos name current models, capacities, rates, removed parameters, and deployment features. Those are mutable. This form tests task-specific evaluations, supplied hypothetical costs, and documented feature contracts instead of making memory of a model catalog the answer. The opened official cache page includes model-dependent rates; none of those current prices are needed to solve this form’s numerical items.
- **Caching is not answer reuse:** Q19 distinguishes an identical stable image prefix from a different inspected image. Prompt caching does not imply that a previous answer is valid for a new request. The official page also states the TTL runs from the request start and refreshes on reads, so a blanket “five minutes after the response finishes” assumption would be wrong. No such timing claim appears in a scored answer.
- **Context preservation:** Token limits are feasibility bounds, not a guarantee of perfect recall. Q17–18 preserve current decisions plus source-version retrieval handles; summaries must be tested on later turns. Tool search defers definitions entering model context, not necessarily definitions supplied to the API; Q31 makes no claim that deferred tool definitions disappear from the request payload.
- **Statistical claims:** A mean latency saving is not a p95 improvement; adding component times does not establish an end-to-end percentile. Q23–24 explicitly require tail validation. Q52 does not invent a confidence interval from marginal totals on paired cases; case-level disagreements and representative additional evaluation are required. Q25 retains selection weights when oversampling slices.
- **GDPR:** Article 22 has an applicability threshold and exceptions. Q48 stipulates counsel’s significant-effects determination and absence of an established Article 22(2) exception. An explanation plus an appeal link does not by itself supply a missing permitted basis. The architect implements the confirmed boundary and routes legal determinations to counsel. Articles 13–15 transparency and Article 22(3) safeguards do not create a universal permission for significant automated decisions.
- **HIPAA:** Q49 stipulates a covered entity and ePHI. HHS explicitly states that maintaining encrypted ePHI without a decryption key can still create business-associate obligations, and distinguishes persistent storage from a narrow transmission-only conduit. Encryption is not automatically HIPAA de-identification. Counsel owns the concrete legal/contractual arrangement; no blanket claim about present eligibility of a particular Claude feature is required.
- **Operational measures:** Q56 reports a met monthly target and a failing enrollment-window experience separately, with explicit denominators. It does not silently relabel the monthly commitment as breached. Q44 distinguishes separate credentials from independent failure domains. Q61 separates headless edit completion from approval to integrate or deploy.
- **Developer contracts:** The opened v1 GitHub Action documentation supports `prompt` and `claude_args`; Q62 checks a controlled migration with permissions and validation. Q60 stipulates server-managed-settings entitlement and administrator access, and requires effective client-policy verification. Organization instructions are context, not an execution-policy enforcement substitute. Hooks were inspected during research but are not used in a scored question; the hook source is omitted from the final form registry accordingly.

## Numerical proofs

All monetary figures are hypothetical scenario inputs, not current provider price quotes. Arithmetic was independently recomputed from the stated denominator and accounting boundary:

1. **Q11:** gross released effort = 80 × 12 = 960 minutes. Correction = 20 × 6 = 120. Net measured release = **840 minutes**. Capacity is not automatically cash savings.
2. **Q12:** A cost per accepted report = $0.018 / 0.90 = **$0.020**. B = $0.026 / 0.98 ≈ **$0.02653**. Total attempt spending includes discarded attempts; both configurations independently pass quality and latency gates.
3. **Q23:** expected avoided time = 0.70 × 2 = 1.40 seconds; all requests pay 0.25 seconds. Mean saving = **1.15 seconds**. This supplies no p95 guarantee.
4. **Q24:** the supplied deterministic illustrative stage total is 5 + 1.4 = **6.4 seconds**, below the eight-second target on that tested workload. The answer still requires measured end-to-end tail performance, rather than claiming a percentile from that sum.
5. **Q52:** old = 40/50 = 80%; new = 42/50 = 84%; observed difference = **4 percentage points** (5% relative). Neither representation proves statistical superiority; paired uncertainty cannot be determined exactly from these marginal totals.
6. **Q53:** $900 + (30,000 × $0.04) + (30,000 × 0.05 × $1.20) = $900 + $1,200 + $1,800 = **$3,900**. Support stays fixed when volume changes.
7. **Q56:** timely enrollment replies = 6,000; timely other replies = 90,000. Monthly timely fraction = 96,000/100,000 = **96%**, exceeding the monthly 95% target while the enrollment window remains at **60%**.

## Originality, validation, and limits

No source-bank question, option set, answer rationale, transcript passage, or old mock question was copied into the public form. General concepts from the four full videos were combined with new industries, evidence boundaries, causal mechanisms, numerical inputs, and credible alternatives. The pre-existing CCAR-P JSON was inspected for its schema only. Drill rows and matching categories were also newly authored; matching choices intentionally repeat.

Mechanical checks pass: 63 unique question IDs; domain counts 11/8/12/10/9/9/4; all 38 objectives; 38 single-choice questions with four options, 21 select-two and four select-three questions with five options; video counts 16/16/16/15; exact chapter-start membership; chapter objective matches; correct-answer cardinality; unique option text within each item; six rectangular all-string exhibits; a deterministic mixed-domain permutation of 0–62; all referenced source keys present; and three five-row unscored drills. Every stem is 55–110 words, option 14–26 words, option rationale 15–35 words, and decision explanation 35–60 words. Answer positions are deterministically shuffled. Correct option lengths vary rather than systematically being longest; substantive qualifiers balance the single-choice length distribution.

The form has not been psychometrically calibrated, validated against actual exam results, or reviewed by the certification owner. Video captions can contain recognition errors; decisive technical answers instead rely on the opened primary references and the explicit assumptions in each stem. Regulatory scenarios are deliberately scoped by an accountable legal owner and are not generic legal advice. Future documentation and feature availability may change; recheck primary references when refreshing the form.
