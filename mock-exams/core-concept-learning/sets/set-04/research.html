Concepts, evidence, credit.

We reviewed complete English captions and chapter metadata for all ten videos. These are materially original scenarios with new constraints and distractors, not transcripts or reproductions of the channel’s questions. Every scored question maps a general concept to an actual source chapter and to supporting documentation. The guide source opens the official certification directory; the supplied July 2026 PDF is not hosted or redistributed here.

The source videos are unofficial practice walkthroughs. Their suggested raw targets do not establish an official passing conversion. Live-exam equivalence is unverified. Product-specific claims were checked against primary sources on 18 October 2026; example rates and configurations are fictional unless explicitly attributed.
