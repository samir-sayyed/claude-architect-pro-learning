    Estimate savings from the cheap model's input-token price and assume the router's declared confidence is calibrated across every case type.

    Price and unvalidated confidence omit system failures.

  3. C · Distractor

    Route all cases cheaply during the test and add stronger-model calls only after customer complaints indicate a missed critical issue.

    It exposes users before validating the critical gate.

  4. D · Distractor

    Exclude escalated cases from both cost and quality metrics because their resolution occurs outside the model-routing service.

    This hides the cost and quality impact being evaluated.

  5. E · Correct

    Include retries, escalations, tool charges and human recovery in cost per completed acceptable outcome, rather than comparing nominal model prices.

    This measures realized system economics.
