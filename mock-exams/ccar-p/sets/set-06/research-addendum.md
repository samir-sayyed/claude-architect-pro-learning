## Arithmetic checked

- **S06Q06:** old human work 12 minutes, new work 4 + 2 = 6. Net monthly capacity = 6,000 × (12 − 4 − 2) / 60 = **600 hours**. Cash/payroll savings do not follow automatically.
- **S06Q38:** three ten-request bursts at minutes 0, 20, 40 force three five-minute writes and 27 reads: 3 × $0.025 + 27 × $0.002 = **$0.129**. A one-hour prefix survives both gaps: one $0.040 write + 29 × $0.002 reads = **$0.098**, saving **$0.031**. No eviction or prefix changes occur; dynamic and output charges are identical and excluded. All charges are fictional. The result depends on workload reuse gaps, not simply a cheaper write price.
